A RISK YOU CAN'T IGNORE

Why EV Fires Are Harder to Control Than You'd Think

Thermal Runaway

A lithium-ion battery keeps generating heat and gas on its own, even after the visible flame has been knocked down.

Ordinary Water Isn't Enough

It typically takes far more water than putting out a conventional vehicle fire, especially in the first few minutes.

Spreads Fast in Enclosed Spaces

Risks spreading to nearby vehicles or the building structure, especially in underground parking.

THE SOLUTION

Trap Heat, Cut Off Oxygen, Stop the Spread

Simply deploy the fire blanket over the affected EV. It traps heat and cuts off oxygen — a key factor in the fire's spread — reducing toxic smoke and limiting radiant heat, creating a safer area for responders to take over.

  • ✓ Ready to deploy immediately — no mechanism or water required
  • ✓ Designed for at least 2 people to pull it fully over the vehicle quickly
  • ✓ PFAS-free, with a 20-year standby storage life

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people does it take to deploy?

It's designed for at least 2 people to pull it fully over the vehicle, using the red pull straps to orient it correctly.

Can it be reused?

It depends on its condition after use. If it wasn't damaged by prolonged direct flame contact and passes inspection, it may be reusable. We recommend having our team check its condition after every use.
